# Healer Deckbuilder — Final Design Review
*Consolidated from five refined tracks (flaws, cards, party, relics, roadmap) · 2026-07-02 · all numbers verified against `src/engine.ts`, `src/constants.ts`, `src/run.ts` @ b20824b*

---

## 1. Executive Summary

1. **The game feels solved because nothing costs anything, not because the dominant line always wins.** Four components — Taunt's free instant retarget, Retain-on-Ability, a Shield that never expires, and the binary Rest gate — form one engine of degeneracy. Restore a cost to each *before* shipping any new content, or every sim baseline and price point in the other four tracks is tuned against a broken meta. This is Phase P1 below.
2. **The boss's real difficulty is an invisible, unwinnable enrage race.** 150 HP vs ~16 dmg/turn means the boss reliably reaches turn-11 enrage; enraged sweep (15 party dmg/turn) vs a ~10 HP/turn sustain ceiling is a strict loss race the UI never announces. Surface the countdown and tune the race in P1 — do not wait for the M8 polish batch where the roadmap originally parked it. All three new bosses assume this fix.
3. **The MP economy gets one structural fix; everything else is opt-in content.** Primary fix: **decaying Rest** (+5 if 0 cards played, +2 if exactly 1). The alternative (delete Rest, +3 MP carryover) is rejected because it destroys Pact of Silence, the restRegen upgrade line, and the Skald's rest-timing tension. Font of Mana, Tithe Candle, Pact of Silence, and Dark Bargain then remain as *build choices*, not repairs. Healer `passiveMp` is hard-capped at +2 total.
4. **Content verdict:** the 14-card set **supersedes** the roadmap's placeholder M6a card list (its Barrier, War Cry, Overcharge, Focus, Sacrifice, Mass Regen are dropped or replaced — details in §3.1). The **relic system overturns the roadmap's cut list** and enters the demo: relic phases 1–2 (plumbing, spoils, Reliquary, 10 legible relics) are committed scope; rule-changers and curses are the designated stretch/cut-line. The **party roster is post-demo**, except the member-identity engine refactor, which lands early so tests migrate once.
5. **Demo shape stands:** 9 fights, 3 acts, 3 bosses (Gatewarden "learn to read," Hexweaver "you can't turtle," Devourer "race or die"), unified sequence P0–P11. The sim harness is committed first; every gate runs at N = 1000 seeds with 95% CIs.
6. **Cross-system safety rule:** one shared **"engine card" tag** (any MP-generating or card-drawing card) is ineligible for Efficient, Retain, *and* First-Light Taper. Without extending the tag to Taper, the relic recreates exactly the free-Inspiration loop the card track's exclusions were built to prevent.
7. **Telegraph integrity is the non-negotiable invariant.** `attackTargets()` promises exact numbers; every damage-modifying card, relic, and boss mechanic must be reflected in the AttackLines preview, enforced by a property test (preview == applied damage) from the first such feature onward.
8. **Sim baselines re-anchor three times:** after P1 (balance repair invalidates the ~100/100/83/67/60 / ~35% baseline), after P6 (full-run band 25–35% tuned against real bosses), and after P9 (relics enter the shop policy).

## 3. Content Proposals

### 3.1 Cross-track resolutions (the big calls)

1. **Relics are IN the demo, overriding the roadmap's cut.** The roadmap cut relics as "cards get there cheaper," but the refined relic set is now the primary delivery vehicle for anti-taunt pressure (#1) and the only track that touches the telegraph system's design space (Dusk Chime, Grounding Rod). The design/critique cost is already paid. Compromise on scope risk: relic implementation phases 1–2 are committed (P9); phases 3–4 (rule-changers, curses) are the demo's designated cut line (Open Q1).
2. **Party roster is POST-demo, upholding the roadmap's cut — with one exception.** 8-choose-3 = 56 comps × 3 new bosses is the largest tuning multiplier in any track. The **member-identity refactor (party M0)** lands in the demo at P2, adjacent to the enemy-def plumbing, so the engine sheds its hardcoded slot identity (`engine.ts:143, 231, 327, 401-403, 467-468, 576, 610-611, 685`) once, before three boss milestones multiply the test surface. The five members, draft flow, and generator constraints ship as the first post-demo phase, waves intact (Open Q2).
3. **The 14-card set supersedes the roadmap's M6a placeholder list.** Specifically: roadmap-**Barrier** (party −1 dmg) → dropped, card-track **Barrier** keeps the name (and Grounding Rod covers party-wide reduction); **War Cry** → superseded by **Battle Hymn**; **Focus** → superseded by **Inspiration**; **Sacrifice** (3 HP → +4 MP) → superseded by **Blood Tithe** (the refined, never-kill, exclusion-tagged version of the same idea); **Overcharge** → cut (the card track adjudicated it as triple-conditional win-more); **Mass Regen** → superseded by **Overgrowth**. **Kept from the roadmap:** **Cleanse** (uncommon, removes Wound — ships with the Hexweaver in P5 so the boss is tuned with its counterplay) and **Second Wind** (heal = missing HP/2, expensive) as an optional 16th card if the pool needs one more body. Roadmap's cut "Bulwark card" is unrelated to the Bulwark *archetype* — no conflict.
4. **One MP-economy fix, everything else is content** (Exec §3): decaying Rest is the repair; Font of Mana / Tithe Candle / Pact of Silence / Prayer Beads / Dark Bargain are builds. Note the card track priced its cheap cards against the current once-per-turn Rest tax; a +2 partial Rest slightly cheapens multi-card turns, so the P7 card sim re-checks Cultivate/Brace/Battle Hymn rates post-P1.
5. **Shield duration (flaw #4) is the substrate for Bulwark.** Base Shield: blocks next instance within 3 enemy turns. Barrier's Shields: 1-phase fuse (strict case of the same rule). Brace/Shield exclusivity and the **Defense → Brace → Shield** damage-pipeline order are built together, previewed correctly in `attackTargets()`.
6. **Naming collisions resolved:** card **Aegis → renamed "Phalanx"** (avoids collision with the relic Aegis Font, whose buffer is named Ward). Martyr's Gift (card) and Martyr's Icon (relic) coexist — different systems, shared theme, acceptable.
7. **Relic acquisition remapped to the 9-fight structure:** spoils picks after the **act-1 and act-2 bosses** (fights 3 and 6), preserving two picks per run; the act-1 spoils roll guarantees ≥1 rare offer; the act-2-boss roll weights act-3-boss counters ×2 and hard-excludes relics dead by then (exclusion list must be recomputed for 9 fights — e.g. Haggler's Charm is no longer boss-dead, since act-3 shops exist; Open Q9). **Reliquary** replaces one rolled node in one mid-act fork of act 2 and of act 3 (exactly twice per run, never in `NODE_TYPE_WEIGHTS`). Relic sim gates re-anchor from the old 35%/60% figures to the P6 bands (full-run 25–35%, boss-3 ~55%).

### 3.2 Card set — 14 cards, 4 archetypes (+ Cleanse, + Second Wind optional)

All join `CARD_POOL` (60/30/10 weights). Anchors: instant single-target healing ≤ ~1.3–1.5 HP/MP, instant AoE ≤ 2.0, anything above must be delayed/conditional/combo; nothing beats poison's 12.5 dmg/MP; Refresh's net +3 is the common-card MP ceiling, +5/+6 the rare ceiling.

**Verdant (HoT/regen engine)** — *"the garden outgrows any wound — if given time."*
- **Overgrowth** — 3 MP, all — uncommon 50g. Regen 1 for 3 turns to every living member (no stack, refresh-duration/keep-stronger). 3.0 HP/MP delayed, truncation claws back ~⅓. **Potent-ineligible.**
- **Cultivate** — 1 MP, single with active Regen — common 25g. +1 Regen strength, +1 duration; modifies **in place** (never an overwrite; never forfeits Lifebloom's burst). ~4–5 HP per MP, dead without a live Regen.
- **Lifebloom** — 3 MP, single — uncommon 50g. Regen 2 for 3 turns; when the Regen **ends while the target lives — expiry, overwrite, or Bloom-consume** — burst-heal 3 (death forfeits). 9 HP / 3 MP delayed.
- **Bloom** — 2 MP, single with active Regen — uncommon 50g. **Consume** the Regen: heal 2 per remaining turn. Regen(2)+Bloom(2) at 3 turns = 6 instant HP / 4 MP = 1.5 combined; Cultivate/Lingering push to 8; Bloom on Lifebloom triggers the burst — the archetype's finisher.

**Bulwark (mitigation, aimed at non-tanks)** — every card **Defense-exclusive**, so the suite protects the members Taunt can't.
- **Brace** — 1 MP, single without Defense — common 25g. Next attack instance −2 (consumed; a member holds Shield **or** Brace, newest replaces). Sweep 4→2 on the edges, aoe 2→0.
- **Phalanx** (was Aegis) — 2 MP, single without Defense — uncommon 50g. Defense 2 for 2 enemy turns; no stack, **cannot target the taunting tank**. Up to 4 prevented / 2 MP; the only scaling answer to sweep spam.
- **Barrier** — 4 MP, all — rare 75g. Every living member gains a Shield; **unused Barrier Shields fade after the next enemy phase**. Into a telegraphed sweep: 12 base / 15 enraged prevented for 4 MP (3.0–3.75); cast blind: mostly wasted.

**Blood (HP-as-resource)** — all three card-inflicted damage effects are **never-lethal** (no new death path in `playCard`).
- **Blood Tithe** — 0 MP, single living >4 HP — uncommon 50g. Target takes 4; gain 2 MP (cap 10). MP-neutral-to-negative on paper; pure tempo. **Retain/engine-tag excluded.**
- **Martyr's Gift** — 2 MP, single living >3 HP — uncommon 50g. Target takes 3; each *other* living member heals 4. Net +5 HP / 2 MP at full value.
- **Grim Harvest** — 2 MP, single living >4 HP — rare 75g. Target takes 4; deal 10 to the enemy. ~2.1 dmg per effective MP — priced for being instant, unconditional, and an enrage-racer.

**Tempo (draw/sequencing/mana/offense)**
- **Battle Hymn** — 1 MP, none — common 25g. Every living member's auto this party phase +2 (+6 for 1 MP full party, degrading 4/2 as members die; lands *this* phase, unlike poison).
- **Inspiration** — 2 MP, none — uncommon 50g. Draw 2. **Efficient/Retain/Taper-excluded** (engine tag).
- **Surge** — 2 MP, none — uncommon 50g. Next card this turn costs 0 (Surge→Surge legal and a strict loss). Profit on 3+ cost, max save Resurrect 6→0.
- **Font of Mana** — 3 MP, none — rare 75g. Refresh 3 for 3 enemy turns (net +6; same status slot as Refresh, no stack, keep-stronger). Equals a double-modded Refresh — the right point for a 75g rare; fights your own Refresh copies for the slot, deliberately.

**Pool structure:** commons 3 (Cultivate, Brace, Battle Hymn — each obsoletes the Quick Heal trap-buy at 25g), uncommons 8, rares 3 (Barrier, Grim Harvest, Font of Mana). Pool 8 → 22 (+Cleanse → 23). No archetype requires three specific cards — each functions from any two plus the starting deck (Bloom works off starting Regen; Brace/Phalanx standalone; Tithe/Gift standalone). Optional follow-up (not required to ship): bias Apothecary/shop offers toward archetypes already in the deck.

### 3.3 Relics — 17, unique, 2–5 per run

Topbar icon row, tooltips. Prices: common 50g / uncommon 75g / rare 100g / cursed 25g (or Shrine "Accept a burden": random curse, +50g; curses never in spoils, never forced). Reliquary stock: 3 relics (60/30/10) + 1 cursed at 25g + removal at 20g (max 2/visit).

**Economy:** **1. Lantern of Foresight** (C, 50g; excluded from pre-final-boss spoils) — hidden attacks reveal their **target** (damage stays unknown; composes with the flaw-#11 baseline, which reveals magnitude). **2. Haggler's Charm** (C, 50g; excluded late) — all shop prices −15g (floor 5); moves affordability thresholds (card rares 75→60, relic rares 100→85, removals 20→5).

**MP engine:** **3. Tithe Candle** (U, 75g) — +1 MP each time a member takes damage **from an enemy attack** (post-Defense/Shield/Ward; 0-damage hits give nothing; no gain at cap — turn-1 usually wastes the trigger). Sweep +3, two +2, single +1: taxes perma-taunt, rewards spreading damage. **4. Pact of Silence** (Cursed) — you can no longer Rest; +1 passive MP/turn (~12 MP/fight vs losing the 5-MP valve and the restRegen line). **5. First-Light Taper** (R, 100g) — first card each turn costs 0 **if effective cost ≤ 2 and it is not an engine-tagged card** (§3.5); draw 1 fewer (hand 3→2). Efficient-modded Heals (3→2) sneak under the gate — deliberate, paid-for; Refresh (3) and Resurrect (6) never qualify.

**Healing:** **6. Aegis Font** (R, 100g) — overhealing from **direct cast heals** converts to Ward (damage buffer, max 4, persists until consumed, larger replaces, independent of Shield, preview-visible). **Regen-tick overheal does NOT convert** — see §3.6.3. **7. Field Triage Kit** (U, 75g) — heals on a member below 50% max HP heal +2 (Quick Heal → 3.0 HP/MP; owns that it also subsidizes the taunt line). **8. Dusk Chime** (R, 100g) — once per fight, a telegraph totaling 6+ is **delayed one turn** (enemy does nothing that phase; same attack marked "Delayed" strikes next turn). A free full turn (11 autos + a card cycle) against the scariest attack.

**Party:** **9. Vanguard's Banner** (R, 100g) — members at full HP deal +2 on their auto (checked per member per phase; ceiling 11→17; one sweep strips it; partial turn-1 uptime is intended tension). **10. War Drum** (U, 75g) — after a member's Ability cast, their next auto deals double (refresh-not-stack; consumed on the next auto). Poison → ranger 8, Bolt → mage 10, Taunt → tank 4 (~0.5/turn, noise — not a taunt subsidy).

**Deck:** **11. Prayer Beads** (C, 50g) — +3 MP on each draw-pile reshuffle (~+3–6/fight at 16 cards; a real engine at 12 via the 20g removal economy). **12. Echo Locket** (U, 75g) — once per fight, the first **Poison or Bolt** Ability returns to hand instead of discarding (MP still paid on replay). Restricted from "any Ability" so it never hands the taunt line a fourth taunt.

**Cursed:** **13. Cracked Hourglass** — enemies enrage on turn 8 instead of 11; Poison and Bolt +2 power (poison 7/tick = 35 total; bolt 7+3×charge, max 22); **Taunt's Defense unaffected** (the race pact cannot be turned into turtle material). Re-cost if poison is nerfed (Open Q5). **14. Rusty Crown** — all enemies +1 attack bonus (boss 0→1; sweep 5 party-wide = 15/turn); fight wins +40g (50→90 — renumber against P3's ~40g/fight economy).

**Boss counters:** **15. Stormglass Mirror** (U, 75g; ×2 weight in pre-final-boss spoils) — once per fight, the first 6+ single-target hit is negated (after Defense; consumed before Shield; preview-visible). **16. Grounding Rod** (C, 50g) — aoe/sweep −1 per hit, applied to the **final** per-hit number (after bonus and enrage ceil), floor 0, preview-visible (enraged sweep ceil(5)−1 = 4).

**Death:** **17. Martyr's Icon** (R, 100g) — first member death each fight becomes survival at 1 HP (statuses cleared, mage charge zeroed; once per fight; wipe check sees the survival).

**Sim gates:** every curse within ±5% of the baseline full-run rate with its intended build; every rare rule-changer (#5, #6, #8, #17) keeps boss winrate within the band (above the ceiling = relic solved the boss, below the floor = trap); relic code inert when `relics` is empty (regression).

### 3.4 Party roster — post-demo phase, design locked now

Pool = 8: the classic trio (Ranger 11/4/Poison, Tank 18/2/Taunt, Mage 9/5/Bolt) plus five:

1. **Hexen Warlock** — 15 HP, **7 dmg** (highest in game). *Soul Tithe:* −2 HP at end of each party phase, after his strike (can kill him). *Dark Bargain* (2 MP): he loses **3 + 3×(prior casts this fight)** HP; healer gains +3 MP. Converts the MP bottleneck into an HP bottleneck; untouched he burns out during turn 8 vs enrage at 11; Potent Regen (2 HP/turn) exactly services the tithe. Constants: `TITHE_AMOUNT 2, BARGAIN_HP_BASE 3, BARGAIN_HP_GROWTH 3, BARGAIN_MP 3`.
2. **Skald** — 10 HP, 0 base; strikes for **2 × cards played this player phase, cap 8**. *Crescendo* (2 MP): draw 2 (counts as 1 card played). Attacks Rest *timing*; makes every cheap card a +2 damage rider.
3. **Berserker** — 16 HP, 2 base; **+2 while HP ≤ 8 + fight attackBonus, +2 more while ≤ 4 + attackBonus** (2/4/6, bands fight-relative, live thresholds on the MemberCard). *Rampage* (2 MP): strike for 2× current damage (+abilityPower), then lose 2 HP — the deliberate band-*entry* tool. Healing him is friendly fire; Quick Heal becomes a precision instrument.
4. **Grave Augur** — 10 HP, 4 dmg. *Grave Chill* (2 MP): the standing telegraph loses 2 (+abilityPower) damage per target (floor 0). Best sweep answer in the game (3.0 HP/MP vs Group Heal's 2.0), bought from the contested Ability economy; the taunt-less draft's redirection substitute. Engine precedent: `_retargetTelegraphToTank` (engine.ts:284–288).
5. **Beast Warden** — 9 HP, 1 dmg. *Call Boar* (2 MP): summon a Boar (7 HP + abilityPower) on her slot for **4 enemy turns** (recast replaces at full; no stack). Boar adds +4 to her strike (total 5) and absorbs every instance aimed at her slot, whole; unhealable, no damage spill. Taunt-protected boar caps at 16 rider damage / 2 MP = 8 dmg/MP < poison's 12.5. Boar HP 7 = exactly one base heavy — the re-summon-early-vs-heal-tank decision is the point.

**Cut:** Blood Cantor (1.3–1.4 dmg/MP vs poison's 12.5 — never opted into), Revenant chassis (Grave Chill salvaged onto the Augur), overheal-as-ammo (banked as a future card mechanic — note Aegis Font now occupies adjacent space; reconcile before reviving it).

**Draft at run start** (not recruit-at-nodes): three 1-of-2 picks from the pool of 8, then slot assignment (L/C/R) on a confirm screen that **surfaces the 25/50/25 primary and 40/20/40 hidden weights**; classic trio always pre-selected, one click to accept. Generator constraints: total conservative damage ≥ 8 with one member ≥ 4 (ratings: Ranger 4, Tank 2, Mage 5, Warlock 7, Berserker 2, Skald 3, Warden 3, Augur 4); ≥ 2 conventionally healable members (assert); taunt-less drafts are intended variance — if taunt-less boss winrate < ~15% in sims, add a purchasable Taunt-lite card rather than forcing the Tank. Deferred: "Wanderer" swap node.

**Waves (post-demo):** W0 identity refactor (pulled into demo P2); W1 Warlock + Skald + minimal pick screen behind a new `draft` `RunLocation`; W2 Berserker + Grave Augur (+ sim policy extensions for band-riding and Chill timing); W3 Beast Warden (pet subsystem — most invasive, ships last); W4 full draft + weight-surfacing UI; W5 per-comp balance pass (keep `ENEMY_ATTACK_BONUS` comp-agnostic; widen boss HP bands if ceiling comps (~16–17 dmg/turn) clear > 85%).

### 3.5 Unified modifier & relic eligibility rules

One shared **engine-card tag** = any MP-generating or card-drawing card (Blood Tithe, Inspiration, Font of Mana, Refresh†, Crescendo post-demo).

1. **Retain:** ineligible for the **Ability card** (flaw #3), all 0-MP cards, and all engine-tagged cards. (Alternative, if Retain feels gutted: retained cards cost +1 MP per re-play.)
2. **Efficient:** cost floor 1 (flaw #19); ineligible for 0-MP cards and Inspiration.
3. **First-Light Taper:** gate is "effective cost ≤ 2 **and not engine-tagged**" — closes the free-Inspiration hole (§3.6.1).
4. **Potent:** ineligible for Overgrowth (18 HP / 3 MP). Potent×Martyr's Gift, Potent/Lingering×Font (net +9, on par with double-modded Refresh at strictly higher gold): allowed, watchlist.
5. **Lingering:** †Refresh either capped at 6 total restored MP per cast or Lingering-ineligible (flaw #7 — pick one in P1). Lingering×Overgrowth (12 delayed AoE HP / 3 MP): allowed, delay + truncation are the brake, watchlist.
6. **Sacrifice-class self-damage cards:** never-lethal HP gates are the rule (all three Blood cards).

### 3.6 Flagged cross-system combos

1. **Taper × Inspiration** — free draw-2 first card every turn. **Broken; fixed** by extending the engine tag to Taper (§3.5.3).
2. **Retain × Brace / Taper × Brace** — a −2 aura for ≤1 MP/turn. Allowed, **playtest watch** (both tracks flagged the Retain half; the Taper half is new).
3. **Aegis Font × Overgrowth/Lifebloom** — regen-tick overheal converting to Ward would print ~3–4 HP/MP of mitigation off a 3-MP AoE. **Broken; fixed:** Ward converts direct cast heals only.
4. **Tithe Candle × Blood cards** — self-inflicted damage must not generate MP. Already excluded by "from an enemy attack"; **add the explicit engine test**.
5. **Field Triage Kit × buffed Quick Heal (heal 2)** — 4.0 HP/MP below 50%. **Sim-gate in P9**; fallback: Triage bonus is +1 for 1-MP heals.
6. **Cracked Hourglass × poison nerf** — if poison goes 5→4 per tick, Hourglass's +2 yields 24 total, not 35; re-cost together (Open Q5).
7. **Prayer Beads × Inspiration × 12-card deck** — intended engine; watch reshuffle MP/fight in sims.
8. **Vanguard's Banner + Battle Hymn** — full-HP burst turn: 11 + 6 + 6 = 23. One-turn, full-HP-gated, both slots paid for; **sim watch**, not a block.
9. **Dusk Chime + Barrier** — a delayed sweep grants a setup turn for a 15-damage Barrier block. Strong, priced (100g + 75g rares); covered by the rare sim gates.
10. **Banked Shields × enrage** — the flaw-#4 duration fix (3 enemy turns) is what keeps pre-enrage Shield-banking bounded; do not ship any enrage tuning before it.
11. **Post-demo previews:** Martyr's Icon × Berserker band-riding, and Aegis Font × any revived overheal mechanic — re-adjudicate in party W2/W5.
